1. Fulfil the existing recorded food obligation as an explicit local meal request, using the existing prototype rice unit (1 kg) and a one-day interval after actual receipt. Do not introduce automatic hunger, death or execution.

2. Consume actual unreserved rice from the custodian household at the holding site. Preserve household ownership and existing salary/treaty reservations; no private money or rice is created. Record an unfulfilled request if free stock is insufficient.

3. Require the original guardian, held person and confirmed current representatives locally; reconfirm after succession. Release closes meal eligibility. Duplicate requests and same-day duplicate receipt cannot consume twice; shortage may be retried after replenishment.

4. Show the witnessed meal receipt or shortage in the existing custody UI. Keep original guarantee, expiry and immutable earlier records. Do not reveal current remote warehouse balances.
