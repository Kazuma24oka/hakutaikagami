# 生前合意による当主職交代と個別承継 V1

家と個人の当事者を分離する最初の工程。現地の生存当主と同意する成人直属家臣が当主職の交代を合意し、家の未払いと運搬の原本は元の所有家へ保持する。新当主が個別原本を確認して精算する。所属・直属・兵・所領・私財・個人的約束は一括転記しない。血統/養子/遺言/死亡/争いの裁定と全国の承継政策は後続。

1. A living incumbent and local consenting adult direct household member nominate and separately confirm a voluntary head-office succession; nomination alone changes no authority and no personal membership/service/troops/property is automatically transferred
2. Runtime head authority uses dated office history across political, administrative, military and new service entry points while initial scenario and past proofs remain unchanged; former head loses fresh head authority
3. House salary debts and outstanding freight retain original owner, principal, receiver, stock and obligation; the new head and relevant counterparty individually acknowledge an original before current-house payment or fixed-site settlement, without forgiving or duplicating debts or cargo
4. Old personal service, active military commands, dated correspondence and prior delegation records are preserved as original agreements rather than rewritten to the successor; pending/unknown remote succession is not shown as live player knowledge
5. Normal entry, stale/duplicate/refused/absent/tampered operations, actual generation recovery/future equality, old version byte compatibility and headless3D data are verified separately from GUI/appearance
